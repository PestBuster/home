Which pests can PestBuster help with?+

We can discuss common issues such as termites, cockroaches, rodents, mosquitoes and bed bugs. Contact us to confirm availability for your specific pest and location.

Is an inspection required before treatment?+

It depends on the pest, property and level of activity. Share the details with our team so we can advise whether an inspection is recommended.

Are treatments safe for children and pets?+

Safety depends on the product and treatment method used. Always follow the technician's preparation, ventilation and re-entry instructions, and tell us about children, pets or sensitivities before service.
